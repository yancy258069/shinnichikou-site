International Trade Business

Engaged in the export trade of food and daily necessities sourced from domestic Japanese legal suppliers.
Product procurement, export, and delivery are conducted according to specific customer needs, following standard international trade processes.

Product Categories

Processed Foods: Instant noodles, snacks, convenient foods, etc.
Seasonings and Food Ingredients: Soy sauce, miso, sauces, spices, etc.
Daily Necessities and Sundries: Kitchenware (tableware, cookware, storage), household cleaning products, sanitary goods (tissues, wipes, etc.)

Product types, specifications, quantities, and packaging can be flexibly arranged according to customer requirements.

Key Partners

  • Overseas trading companies
  • Wholesalers and distributors

Cooperation and Trade Process

Requirement Communication: Customers submit procurement requirements

Procurement and Confirmation: The company arranges procurement based on customer needs

Settlement and Payment: Customers complete payment in the agreed currency and method

Export and Logistics: The company arranges shipping, customs clearance, and delivery

All transactions strictly comply with the laws and regulations of Japan and relevant countries. The procurement, export, and settlement processes are transparent, compliant, and traceable.
