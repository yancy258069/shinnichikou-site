Mission & Philosophy

Shinhiki Sangyo Co., Ltd. is dedicated to providing reliable import and export services and business cooperation support to global clients, building long-term and stable international partnerships through standard and sound operations.

We uphold the principles of integrity, steady development, and win-win cooperation. We place high importance on compliance management and risk control, ensuring that all business activities strictly comply with relevant laws, regulations, and industry standards.

Our vision is to leverage local resources to deliver high-quality trade, consulting, and dining operation services, promoting the safe and sustainable development of cross-border business.

Our Strengths

  • Rigorous selection and evaluation of formal suppliers and manufacturing plants to ensure quality and stable supply.
  • Construction of an efficient supply chain and project management system, focusing on process transparency and on-time delivery.
  • Provision of flexible customized solutions to fully meet the diverse business needs of our clients.
  • Strict adherence to local compliance and risk management requirements, committed to building safe and reliable partnerships.

Core Business

1. International Trade: Import and export trade of commodities (food, daily necessities, etc.).
2. Business Consulting & Market Research: Feasibility studies, regional environmental assessments, and on-site proxy services.
3. F&B Operations: Pre-opening planning and daily operational management of physical restaurant stores.

We offer flexible business matching tailored to client requests, striving for standardized operation across all stages. For details, please refer to the Services page.
