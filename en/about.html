Mission and Philosophy

Shinnichikou Sangyo Co., Ltd. is dedicated to providing reliable export services for Japanese goods to global clients, establishing long-term and stable international partnerships through standardized and steady trade operations.

We adhere to the principles of honest management, steady development, and win-win cooperation, placing high importance on compliance and risk control to ensure all trade activities meet relevant laws, regulations, and international trade practices.

Our vision is to become a trusted supplier of Japanese food and daily necessities for overseas customers, promoting the safe and sustainable distribution of high-quality Japanese products in the international market.

Our Advantages

  • Strict selection and evaluation of domestic Japanese legal suppliers to guarantee product quality and stable supply
  • Establishment of an efficient supply chain management system to ensure on-time delivery
  • Flexible procurement and supply solutions to support diverse customer requirements
  • Transparent transaction processes and strict adherence to compliance requirements for a safe and reliable partnership

Core Business

Procurement and export trade of domestic Japanese products
International supply of processed foods, seasonings, and daily necessities
Trade cooperation and business support for overseas enterprises

Based on customer needs, our company provides diverse product combinations and strives for standardized operations throughout the entire process of procurement, export, and logistics.
