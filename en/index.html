Company Profile

Shinnichikou Sangyo Co., Ltd. is a comprehensive trading enterprise established in Japan, specializing in the procurement and export of domestic Japanese food products and daily necessities.

Based on legally compliant trade processes, we provide high-quality Japanese-origin product export services to overseas partners through standardized business operations and supply chain management.
