Company Profile

Headquartered in Kobe, Japan, Shinhiki Sangyo Co., Ltd. is a comprehensive commercial service enterprise rooted in the Kansai region and connected globally.

Centering on international trade, we conduct import and export businesses (such as food and daily necessities),
while leveraging local resources to provide professional business consulting and market research services.
We are also actively expanding into F&B operations, committed to delivering high-quality, all-around services to our clients and consumers.
