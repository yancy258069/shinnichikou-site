International Trade

We engage in import and export trade, primarily covering food products and daily necessities. Product procurement, import/export, and delivery are executed based on specific client requirements in accordance with standard international trade processes.

【Product Categories】

Processed Foods: Instant noodles, snacks, convenience foods, etc.
Condiments & Food Ingredients: Soy sauce, miso, sauces, spices, etc.
Daily Necessities & Household Goods: Kitchenware, household cleaning supplies, hygiene products, etc.

Product types, specifications, quantities, and packaging can be arranged according to client requirements.

【Main Partners】

  • Domestic and international trading companies
  • Wholesalers and distributors

【Trade Process】

Requirement Communication: Clients submit procurement or import/export requests

Procurement & Confirmation: Arrange product selection and confirmation with formal suppliers/factories

Settlement & Payment: Complete payment according to agreed currency and payment methods

Logistics & Customs: Arrange warehousing, customs clearance, and shipping distribution
